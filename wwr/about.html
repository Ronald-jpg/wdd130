History

White-water rafting in Uganda began in 1996 when Adrift pioneered the first descent of the Victoria Nile in Jinja after scouting trips in 1986 and 1995. Centered on the Nile near Jinja, often called the "Adventure Capital of East Africa," the industry grew rapidly, attracting over 40,000+ tourists for Class III-V rapids, although hydroelectric dam developments like Bujagali (2011) and Isimba have submerged some legendary rapids.

Adventure Awaits You

Uganda, the "Pearl of Africa," offers unparalleled, high-adrenaline adventures, including mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, white-water rafting the Nile's Grade 5 rapids in Jinja, and chimpanzee trekking
