Nile White Water Rafting

White water rafting on the Nile in Jinja, Uganda, is a world-class adventure offering thrilling Class IV and V rapids, such as Hypoxia and Jaws, along with calmer stretches for scenery viewing. Operating year-round, these 3-5 hour trips (spanning 15-31 km) are accessible to beginners and require no prior experience, supported by highly trained safety teams

History

White water rafting on the Nile began in the early 1990s near Jinja, Uganda, transforming the Source of the Nile into a premier global adventure destination..

Early exploration showed that despite the construction of dams, the section between Jinja and Lake Kyoga offered world-class, high-volume whitewater..
